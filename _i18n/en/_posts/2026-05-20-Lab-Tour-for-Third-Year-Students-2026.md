We recently held a tour of the Lee Lab for third-year students, where we demonstrated robots and intelligent spaces. 
Participants enjoyed seeing the robots in action and observing how objects respond to people’s movements. 
Future lab tours will be held on the following dates

・June 4, 2:55 PM – 4:30 PM
・June 5, 4:40 PM – 6:15 PM

If you’re even slightly interested in our research or are still deciding where to join, please come visit us!
