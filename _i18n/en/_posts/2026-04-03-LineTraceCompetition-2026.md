The Line Trace Competition and the Spring Workshop Certificate Presentation Ceremony were recently held!
During the spring workshop, the new fourth-year students who are planning to go on to graduate school each worked on building their own line-trace cars.
On the day of the event, an exciting time competition took place on a special course created by the professor.
Among them, Hanai won first place!
Congratulations!
